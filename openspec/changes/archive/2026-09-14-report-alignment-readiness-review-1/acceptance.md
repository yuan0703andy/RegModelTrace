# Acceptance

- All certified metrics derive from saved identity-bound truth and predictions; no inference runs occur.
- RMS/ARA, Impact, CoreLogic/Cotality, Verisk, and KCC v2.2 results are reported separately with primary/control denominators.
- Sufficiency, relation, and joint matrices conserve eligible, invalid, and missing cases.
- Undefined and untested class metrics are machine-readable nulls with reasons.
- Frozen proper scores and formulas are carried forward without thresholding, clipping, or refitting.
- RAG claims distinguish ID/span resolution, semantic citation support, retrieval recall, claim precision, issue recall, and causal safety.
- SQLite inventory uses read-only connections and excludes FTS shadow rows from evidence counts.
- Storage counts distinguish logical memberships, unique content, physical copies, and model caches.
- The LoRA memo preserves `FINE_TUNING = NOT_JUSTIFIED` for the closed release and grants no training authorization.
- Sequential audit actions preserve requirement logic, source roles, access conditions, human approval, and unresolved stopping states.
- Historical artifacts are unchanged after reporting.
- Final state is `REVIEW_1 = COMPLETE` or `COMPLETE_WITH_LIMITATIONS`, `REPORTING_ONLY = TRUE`, `NEW_INFERENCE_RUNS = 0`, `TRAINING_AUTHORIZATION = NONE`.
