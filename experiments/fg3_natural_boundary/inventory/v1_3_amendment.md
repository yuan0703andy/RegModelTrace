# FG-3 v1.3 prospective protocol amendment

This amendment was frozen before substantive screening of the 85 selected page leads. It does not rerun discovery or change the 1,708 query/page hits, 85 leads, v1.2 case-formation tiers, historical FG-2 scores, model settings, or human truth. No natural model inference occurred.

The amendment fixes nine measurement boundaries: FG-4 retrieval eligibility is independent of FG-3 semantic accuracy; evidence packets are mechanically built and hash-frozen before human adjudication; packet omission, source indeterminacy, and human disagreement are distinct; Panel B reports the human disagreement baseline; Panel C detects an explicit **documentary statement** of a review-linked same-object model modification, not independently proved causation; H1 uses directional dependency-cluster replication; the sampling frame is cue-defined; H4 is limited to two pinned model configurations; and primary exposure eligibility is operationalized under `KNOWN_EXPOSURE_LEDGER_V1`.

The exposure ledger inventories 347 in-scope textual files by path and SHA-256. It records protected KCC Test E content as excluded; KCC sealed truth and raw source were not opened. Lack of a ledger match will not prove that a passage was never seen outside the frozen ledger. Lead-level matching has not begun.

The negative-frame rule targeted 24 no-hit pages, four per document. The fixed rule yielded **21** pages: 2021 reviewer PDF had only two no-hit pages and 2023 reviewer PDF had only three; the other four PDFs contributed four each. Selected IDs and ranks were frozen from existing hit metadata without page-content review. Human `YES / NO / AMBIGUOUS` labeling remains pending. These pages are a frame diagnostic and cannot enter the primary model set.

The v1.2 case-formation JSON retains its original checksum; the v1.3 Panel C label override is documented in the protocol without changing pairing or selection logic. The complete checksums and historical comparison are in `../protocol/v1_3_amendment_receipt.json`.

Next gate: Checkpoint 2 source-only exposure audit and original-PDF verification of the unchanged 85 leads. No model run is authorized by this amendment.
