The source-only script processed the 2021/2023 standards, Florida Public submissions, and Professional Team reports. The complete hit ledger, selected lead registry, document page counts, query counts, and file hashes are under [`candidates/`](../candidates/). A second identical run produced byte-identical outputs.

These are **page-level lexical leads, not 85 natural cases**. No lead has yet been checked against the full original PDF context, paired across source roles, or assessed for human evidence sufficiency. The snippets are `pdftotext` output and are explicitly marked unverified. The selected lead on physical page 7 of the 2023 Professional Team report was inspected during the earlier exploratory inventory and is marked `DEVELOPER_EXPOSED`. The other 84 remain `EXPOSURE_UNKNOWN`, not prospectively cleared. The [`exposure_audit.jsonl`](exposure_audit.jsonl) records the known exposure and lists outstanding case-level checks for every selected lead.

The first checkpoint is therefore **prepared, not passed**. Its remaining work is full source-context verification, cross-document pairing where needed, and exact/substantially-equivalent prior-use review. Only then can an actual candidate case registry and frozen model-facing evidence packets be formed. Neither human truth nor a natural Qwen run exists at this point. FG-2, the Qwen3.8 synthetic diagnostic, the active Qwen2.5 default, and protected historical evaluations were not changed.

There is a substantive risk that the two Florida Public cycles yield few independent cases after exposure and exact-context screening. That outcome is allowed by the protocol; the lead count must not be treated as sample size or used to imply the planned panels are feasible.

The 2026-09-23 inventory's Qwen3.8 download/runtime paragraph is a dated historical note and is superseded by the later synthetic diagnostic. This checkpoint uses that inventory only for PDF identity and exposure bookkeeping; it makes no new claim about model readiness or quality.
