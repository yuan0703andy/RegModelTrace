The Panel A whole-section rule remains separate. A heading locates context; it does not cause every child paragraph to become a Panel C match. This is the narrower reading of the already frozen phrase “identifier appearing in both units.” The cost is bounded-search miss risk for relevant content documented only under an inherited section heading. That risk is recorded, not converted into a corpus-absence claim.
